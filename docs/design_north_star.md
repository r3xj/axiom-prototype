# AXIOM Design North Star

AXIOM is a single-player fantasy grand strategy game about discovering the hidden rules of a generated world and adapting strategy around them.

## Core Fantasy

The player begins in a world they do not fully understand. Materials, factions, gods, terrain, and ancient powers may behave differently each game. The player wins by investigating the world, building logistics around what they discover, and adapting to asymmetric pressure.

## Permanent Design Rules

1. The world is a mystery, not a menu.
   - Do not present the player with perfect information immediately.
   - Discovery, testing, scouting, research, and field use should matter.

2. Everything is physical and local.
   - No global inventory unless explicitly added later.
   - Supplies, ore, equipment, caravans, squads, mines, and projects exist in places.
   - Goods must move physically between places.

3. Population is the root resource.
   - Labor and military manpower come from people.
   - Losing soldiers should eventually matter to the settlement’s future.
   - Do not treat units as free abstract cards.

4. Supplies are concrete goods.
   - Supplies support work, remote sites, squads, guard posts, mines, and future armies.
   - Supply routes are pressure points.

5. Materials are discovered, not known.
   - A material’s first description should not reveal its optimal use.
   - Properties should emerge through survey, testing, research, crafting, and field use.
   - Long-term, material behavior should be systems-driven, not hard-coded item bonuses.

6. Security is projected presence.
   - Territory is not safe just because it is colored as owned.
   - Scouts, squads, guard posts, patrols, roads, and supply lines create real control.

7. Pressure should be actor-driven and diegetic.
   - Raiders, neighbors, deities, and Legacy Empires should act in the world.
   - Avoid abstract “threat meters” as the player-facing explanation.

8. The prototype must stay scoped.
   - Phase 1A is non-combat discovery/logistics only.
   - Do not add raiders, combat, AI, trade, specialists, gods, diplomacy, or procedural generation until explicitly asked.

## Current Prototype Direction

Phase 1A proves:
inspect map → survey prospect → confirm material → claim deposit → establish mine → supply mine → mine ore → ship ore → test/analyze → update Codex → complete first material output.

The goal is not to build the full game yet. The goal is to prove that discovery, local stockpiles, labor, supplies, projects, and material knowledge form a playable loop before adding raiders or combat.
